Presence Simulator — schedule preview

A real week of away mode, replayed in your browser from a learned schedule captured by presence_simulator.export_schedule. The controls are the same ones the integration exposes on its device page; move them and the whole week re-simulates.

Every control re-runs the week against the same random stream, so a change you see is caused by the control and not by luck. Re-roll draws a fresh stream with the settings unchanged — the simulation is deliberately random, so no two weeks match.

The decision logic here is a faithful JavaScript port of model.next_state, model.apply_variation and the per-slot step in coordinator._run_step — deadband, quiet-hours rule, sticky on/off chain, max-concurrent trim, minimum dwell and timing jitter, in that order. Because Python and JavaScript have different random number generators the exact week differs from a real run, but the behaviour and the statistics do not.

Schedule resolution is fixed at 15 minutes here because that is the resolution the bundled learned data was captured at; in Home Assistant it is set during configuration and changing it starts a separate model.
